# Sample Policy: Flexible Working Hours

This is a template an organization deploying WorkWell can adapt. Employees are encouraged to
structure their working hours around a core collaboration window (for example, 10:00-15:00 local
time) while retaining flexibility over the remainder of the day. Managers should evaluate output
and outcomes rather than hours logged or time-of-day activity.

When WorkWell surfaces a sustained after-hours or weekend-work pattern, the intended response
under this policy is a private nudge to the employee, not an automatic report to their manager.
If an employee's own workload consistently requires after-hours work to stay on track, that is
treated as a staffing or scoping signal for their manager to address in a normal 1:1, using the
employee's own account of the work - not the raw telemetry.
