# Sample Policy: Right to Disconnect

This is a template an organization deploying WorkWell can adapt. Employees are not expected to
monitor or respond to non-urgent work communication outside their agreed working hours. Sending a
message outside those hours should default to scheduled delivery where the tool supports it, to
avoid creating an implicit expectation of an immediate reply.

WorkWell's shutdown-reminder intervention exists to support this policy at the individual level:
it is a private, dismissible nudge shown to the employee themselves when their own after-hours
activity has risen and stayed elevated, never a compliance mechanism reported upward.
