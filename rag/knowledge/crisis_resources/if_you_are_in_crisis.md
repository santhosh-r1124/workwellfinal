# If You Are in Crisis: Immediate Resources

If you are in immediate physical danger, contact your local emergency number right away.

If you are having thoughts of suicide or self-harm, or are in significant emotional distress, you
do not have to handle it alone:

- In the United States, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), available 24/7.
- Outside the United States, contact your local emergency number or search for a national crisis
  line for your country - many countries have a free, 24/7 equivalent service.
- If your employer offers an Employee Assistance Program (EAP), it typically provides free,
  confidential counseling and can be reached directly, without going through a manager.

WorkWell's automated analysis of work patterns (schedule, breaks, focus) is never used by itself
to declare a crisis. If you tell WorkWell's assistant something that sounds like a safety concern,
it will point you to resources like these rather than trying to interpret it on its own.
