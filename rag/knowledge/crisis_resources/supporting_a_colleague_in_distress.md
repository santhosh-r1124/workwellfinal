# Supporting a Colleague in Distress

If a colleague seems to be struggling, the most useful first step is usually a direct, private,
non-judgmental question - "I've noticed you seem stretched thin lately, how are you actually
doing?" - rather than guessing or diagnosing. Listen without trying to immediately fix the
problem, and let them lead on what kind of support they want.

You are not expected to be a mental health professional. If they mention thoughts of self-harm or
suicide, take it seriously, stay with them or help them contact someone who can (a crisis line, a
trusted person, emergency services if there is immediate danger), and avoid promising to keep it a
secret if their safety is at risk. Encourage them to use their employer's Employee Assistance
Program if one is available, and follow up with them again in the following days - a single
conversation is a start, not a resolution.
