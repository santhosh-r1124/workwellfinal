# Setting a Workday Shutdown Ritual

A brief, consistent end-of-day routine helps signal to your own attention system that work is
over, which supports psychological detachment and next-day recovery. A simple version: review
tomorrow's top priority, close work applications and tabs, and say (out loud or to yourself) a
short closing phrase like "shutdown complete." This sounds small, but consistency matters more
than the specific content of the ritual.

If evening work is unavoidable on a given day, keeping it time-boxed (a defined start and stop
time, decided in advance rather than open-ended) preserves most of the recovery benefit compared
to open-ended after-hours work.
